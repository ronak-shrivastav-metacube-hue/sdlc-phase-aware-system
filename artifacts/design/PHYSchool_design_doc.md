# Technical Design Outline

**Project:** PHYSchool  
**Client:** JP Morgen  
**Date:** 2026-10-17  

## Feature Scope:
Create ER digram for student management system

## Architecture & Design Specifications:
1. Identify key entities such as students, courses, instructors, and grades to be included in the ER diagram.
2. Determine the relationships between entities, such as a student enrolling in courses, an instructor teaching courses, and grades being assigned to students.
3. Define the attributes for each entity, such as student ID, course name, instructor name, and grade value.
4. Validate the ER diagram with stakeholders to ensure it accurately represents the requirements of the student management system.
