1. Create wireframes that clearly outline the user flow and layout of the checkout page, including all necessary fields and buttons.

3. Ensure consistency with the overall brand aesthetic and design guidelines to maintain a cohesive look and feel throughout the checkout process.
4. Collaborate with stakeholders and gather feedback to iterate on the wireframes and make necessary adjustments before moving on to the next phase of development.
